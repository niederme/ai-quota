# Approved splash artwork

Source: https://www.figma.com/design/cja43k0cJmCFUnGctkDuJ2/AIQuota?node-id=502-17

Approved September 22, 2026. Editable vectors and glass effects live in icon-1 on Page 1.
Export the transparent frame as PNG at widths 420, 840, and 1260 for LaunchArtwork 1x/2x/3x. The 1024px master is preserved here. Both appearance slots currently use the approved artwork unchanged.

The app icon and onboarding tile remain Icon Composer renders. The splash background is separate. generate-ios-splash.py only regenerates the background; it must not replace the approved foreground.

The centered square frame uses 80% of viewport width, capped at 420pt and 80% of viewport height. Transparent source padding makes the visible mark about 57% of phone viewport width.
The artwork center is 48% down the viewport, an optical offset 2% above the geometric center.
