FAQ

Questions?

The quick answers on tracking, sign-in, privacy, widgets, and getting set up on your Mac, iPhone or iPad.

What does AIQuota actually track?

AIQuota shows your Codex and Claude quota windows, reset times, and warning states in the Mac menu bar, the iPhone and iPad app, and widgets. You can see at a glance whether you are in the clear, getting close, or already capped.

Do I need to connect both Codex and Claude?

No. You can use AIQuota with Codex only, Claude only, or both. If you only connect one service, the app and widgets adapt to that setup instead of leaving empty space for a second one.

How does sign-in work?

On Mac, AIQuota uses your existing ChatGPT and Claude browser-backed sessions. On iPhone and iPad, you sign in to each service inside the app. You do not need to paste in API keys. Credentials are stored in Keychain on each device and shared only with AIQuota’s widgets.

Do my Mac and iPhone share a connection?

No. Each device connects to Codex and Claude on its own, and readings are not synced between devices.

Does AIQuota read my prompts or code?

AIQuota is built to monitor quota status, not inspect your conversations. It needs account access to fetch usage information, but it does not need your prompt history to show gauges, reset timing, and notifications. You can read more in the Privacy Policy.

Does AIQuota collect usage analytics?

Not by default. AIQuota includes an optional setting called “Share anonymous usage data,” and it starts off on Mac, iPhone and iPad. If you turn it on, AIQuota sends simple anonymous metrics: app launch, daily active use, app version, service connection, and onboarding completion. These events may be processed by Google Firebase Analytics. No prompts, tokens, cookies, or personal info.

Do widgets and alerts cover both services?

Yes. On Mac there are small, medium, and large desktop widgets. On iPhone and iPad there are four Home Screen layouts and Lock Screen widgets, for one service or both. Alerts can let you know when quota is low, at the limit, or reset, so you are not caught off guard mid-session.

What do I need to run it?

AIQuota for Mac requires macOS 15 or later: download the notarized build, move it to Applications, and follow the guided setup. AIQuota for iPhone and iPad requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
