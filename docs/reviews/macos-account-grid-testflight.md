# macOS account grid TestFlight check

This internal macOS beta keeps the existing combined menu-bar gauge treatment. The open panel uses two columns when 600 points of width are available and one column on narrower displays. With odd account counts, the last card stays on the left. The viewport scrolls when the available screen height cannot show every row.

## Test on a Mac

1. Connect one through six accounts using distinct names, including a long name. In Settings, add accounts by naming them before sign-in. Check that every account surface remains inside the panel and that its plan, usage, reset, loading, and error text remain readable. Compare the dial, plan and reset hierarchy with the accepted iOS 2.0 overview.
2. Reorder accounts by dragging, the card context menu, and VoiceOver's Move earlier/later actions. Close and reopen the panel; verify the order persists. For three and five accounts, confirm the final card stays in the left column.
3. Click an account to open details. Confirm there are no permanent Details or Manage buttons, repeated service labels, or per-account update timestamps. Refresh and management remain in the secondary context menu and Settings. Resize display resolution or use a smaller laptop screen; watch for jumps or clipped Settings/Quit controls.
4. In onboarding, select the accounts for the menu bar. Up to two should be selected initially. In Settings, select three or more, clear all selections, and reconnect or add an account. The single combined menu-bar item should show the selected gauges, or the app icon for an empty selection. New accounts should not be automatically selected after an explicit choice; panel reordering should not change this list.
5. Add a small or single-service medium widget, choose a specific account in widget configuration, and compare its values with the matching panel card. Changing the menu-bar selection must not change the widget's account. Existing service-only widget configurations should continue to work.
6. While Codex has no reading and is loading, confirm its account surface shows only Codex loading state. Claude's monthly spend must never appear there. Also inspect an account with a saved reading and a refresh error: the saved metric and a concise error state should remain visible.
7. With one or two accounts, compare the open panel's native bottom edge with the footer. It should hug the footer without a large blank strip. Check again after loading finishes, opening and closing details, and moving between displays. In the flat two-column view, percentages and periods should occupy separate dial lines; plan names remain prominent, spending text remains legible, and reset captions read naturally.
8. Hover each account region and move the pointer away: only the active region should receive a subtle highlight. Tab to each account and open it with Return or Space; keyboard focus should remain visible. There should be no small disclosure chevron. Repeatedly open and close details while an account loads or refreshes. Compare the native window's top and horizontal edges before, during, and after navigation; the detail page should keep the overview width, including with one account and on a narrower display.
9. In Settings, find Add Codex account beneath Codex and Add Claude Code account beneath Claude Code. Open each naming sheet and confirm its service name, focus and empty-name Connect state; Cancel must leave the account list unchanged. Rename an existing account afterward to check the two presentation paths independently. Complete sign-in only with an authorized test account, then confirm the new account appears under its service and routes to its own usage.

The static comparison widgets and service-level alerts still use the original provider connections. Additional account detail has summary metrics, not the complete historical analytics of the original provider detail. Live window anchoring, multi-account browser sign-in, and sandboxed widget refresh need hands-on TestFlight verification.
