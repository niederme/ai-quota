Two widgets, every size

Six kinds collapse to two: Account for one account in depth, Accounts for several at a glance. Either can pick accounts by hand or follow whichever is closest to its limit.

01

Accounts, not services

Every widget takes an ordered list of accounts from any service, or Automatic: the account closest to a limit.

02

The app’s dial, unchanged

Glyph, both readings the same size, the worst window colours the whole dial. Tokens only, no hand-mixed purple.

03

Used, everywhere

Percent used, the same as the app. Reset captions in reset-accent. No “left” rows.

04

History earns its space

Medium and large add the token heatmap; large adds the weekly split. History never repeats a number the dial shows.

05

Brand ground, accentable

Full colour sits on base and wash. Tinted and clear mark rings accentable and keep text primary.

Migration

Reuse kind strings where the family matches so placed widgets survive: CodexLockScreen and ServiceDetails become Account, AIQuotaHomeSingle becomes Account, AIQuotaHomeBoth becomes Accounts. BothLockScreen and CompactLockScreen map to Accounts rectangular with their two accounts prefilled. Worth a device check before committing.
