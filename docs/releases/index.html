AIQuota 2.0

AIQuota comes to iPhone and iPad.

  • AIQuota for iPhone and iPad. Codex and Claude on dual-ring cards, with reset times, plan details and reading age.
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Four Home Screen layouts for one service or both, and configurable Lock Screen widgets.
  • A refreshed Mac app. Personal and work accounts on cards you can reorder, with your choice of menu bar gauges.
  • Codex usage history. Daily usage in the Codex card, with model and app breakdowns in its details.
  • Codex reset announcements. A dismissible notice when Codex Resets reports an announcement.
  • Every kind of allowance. Monthly and weekly-only plans fill a single gauge; missing windows are left out.
  • Notifications at your threshold. One alert per window at the level you choose, with optional reset reminders.
  • Try demo. Explore sample usage on iPhone and iPad without signing in.
